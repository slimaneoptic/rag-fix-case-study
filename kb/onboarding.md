# Getting Started with Orbitask

## 1. Create your workspace

Sign up at app.orbitask.example with a work email. The person who creates the workspace becomes the workspace owner. A workspace can have up to 3 owners.

## 2. Invite your team

Invite users under Settings > Members, by email or by sharing an invite link. Invite links expire after 7 days. Paid plans are billed for every active user; guests who can only view are free.

## 3. Import your work

You can import projects from CSV files, Trello, Asana and Jira. Imports of up to 10,000 tasks finish in a few minutes; larger imports run in the background and you get an email when they are done. The Jira importer keeps comments and attachments; the Trello importer keeps comments but not attachments.

## 4. Set up automations

Automations run actions when something happens, for example "when a task is marked Done, post a message in Slack". Each action that runs counts as one automation run toward your plan's monthly limit.
