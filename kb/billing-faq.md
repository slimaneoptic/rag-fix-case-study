# Billing FAQ

**Which payment methods do you accept?**
Visa, Mastercard and American Express cards on all plans. Annual plans with a yearly total above $5,000 can also pay by invoice with bank transfer (net 30 days).

**In which currencies can I pay?**
US dollars (USD) or euros (EUR). The currency is set at the first purchase and cannot be changed later.

**Do you charge tax?**
VAT is added for customers in the European Union unless a valid VAT number is entered. US sales tax is added in the states where it applies.

**What happens if a payment fails?**
We retry the card 3 times over 10 days and email the workspace owners each time. If all retries fail, the workspace becomes read-only until the payment is made. Data is not deleted while the workspace is read-only.

**What happens when I add users in the middle of a billing period?**
New users are charged pro rata for the rest of the current period, then at the full price from the next renewal.

**Can I get a refund?**
See the Refund Policy for refund windows and how to request one.

**When will I receive my invoice?**
Invoices are emailed to the billing contact on the day of each charge and are available under Settings > Billing > Invoices.

**Can I change from monthly to annual billing?**
Yes, at any time. The switch takes effect immediately and the unused part of the current month is credited.
