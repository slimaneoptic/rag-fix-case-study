# Customer Support

## Support channels and hours

| Plan | Channels | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | Email | Monday to Friday, 08:00-18:00 UTC |
| Team | Email and chat | Monday to Friday, 08:00-18:00 UTC |
| Business | Email, chat and phone | 24 hours a day, Monday to Friday |
| Enterprise | Email, chat, phone and a named support manager | 24/7 |

## First response targets by severity

| Severity | Example | Business | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Critical | Workspace is down for everyone | 4 hours | 1 hour |
| High | A key feature is broken, no workaround | 8 hours | 4 hours |
| Normal | Question or minor issue | 1 business day | 8 hours |

Starter and Team requests are answered in order of arrival, within the reply times listed on the Plans and Pricing page.

## Escalation

If a critical issue is not solved within 24 hours, Business and Enterprise customers can ask for an escalation to the support lead by replying "escalate" in the ticket.
