# Service Level Agreement (SLA)

## Uptime commitment

| Plan | Monthly uptime commitment |
|---|---|
| Starter | No commitment (best effort) |
| Team | No commitment (best effort) |
| Business | 99.9% |
| Enterprise | 99.95% |

Uptime is measured each calendar month for the Orbitask web app and API, by external monitoring from three regions.

## Service credits

If monthly uptime falls below the commitment for your plan, you can claim a service credit, calculated as a percentage of that month's subscription fee:

| Monthly uptime | Service credit |
|---|---|
| Below the commitment but at least 99.0% | 10% |
| Below 99.0% but at least 95.0% | 25% |
| Below 95.0% | 50% |

Service credits are capped at 50% of the monthly fee and are added to your account balance. They are not paid out as cash refunds.

## How to claim

Open a support ticket within 30 days after the end of the affected month, with the dates and times of the outage. Credits are confirmed within 10 business days.

## Exclusions

Uptime does not count:
- Scheduled maintenance, announced at least 72 hours in advance, limited to 4 hours per month and done on Sundays between 02:00 and 06:00 UTC.
- Problems caused by the customer's own network, integrations or third-party services.
- Features labelled Beta.
