# Release Notes 2026

## June 2026

- New: recurring tasks can now repeat on the last working day of the month.
- Improved: search results load about twice as fast.

## April 2026

- New refund policy: annual plans can now be refunded within 30 days (was 14 days), and monthly plans within 7 days of the first payment. See the Refund Policy.
- Refund requests are now made in the app instead of by email.

## March 2026

- Pricing update: the Team plan now includes 10,000 automation runs per month (was 5,000), and the Business plan 50,000 (was 25,000). Prices did not change.
- Business plan storage raised from 1 TB to 2 TB per workspace.

## January 2026

- API v2 released, with higher rate limits on all plans. API v1 will stop working on December 31, 2026.
- API keys now expire after 365 days.
