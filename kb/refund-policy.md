# Refund Policy

This policy applies to all Orbitask subscriptions bought or renewed on or after April 1, 2026. It replaces the refund policy dated January 15, 2024.

## Annual plans

You can cancel an annual plan and get a full refund if you ask within 30 days of the start of the annual term (the purchase or renewal date). After 30 days, cancelling stops the next renewal, the workspace stays active until the end of the paid term, and the unused months are not refunded.

## Monthly plans

Monthly plans can be refunded within 7 days of the first payment of a new subscription. Later monthly payments are not refunded; cancelling stops the next charge.

## Downgrades and removed users

If you move to a cheaper plan or remove users in the middle of a billing period, the unused amount is added to your account as a prorated credit. The credit is applied automatically to your next invoices. Credits cannot be paid out as cash.

## How to request a refund

Workspace owners request refunds in the app under Settings > Billing > Request a refund. Requests from other users are not accepted.

## Processing time and fees

Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method within 5 business days. There is no fee for refunds, including refunds of payments made by bank transfer.

## What is never refunded

- Add-ons already used during the billing period (for example extra storage).
- Accounts closed for breaking the Acceptable Use Policy.
- Service credits under the SLA, which are given as account credit only.
