# Plans and Pricing

Orbitask is sold per user (seat). Prices are in US dollars and do not include taxes. Annual billing is charged once a year for the whole term; the annual prices below are shown per user per month so they can be compared with monthly billing.

## Plan comparison

| Plan | Monthly billing (per user / month) | Annual billing (per user / month) | Minimum users | Storage per workspace | Automation runs per month | Support |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $12 | $10 | 1 | 50 GB | 1,000 | Email, reply within 2 business days |
| Team | $24 | $20 | 3 | 500 GB | 10,000 | Email and chat, reply within 1 business day |
| Business | $39 | $33 | 10 | 2 TB | 50,000 | Priority support, reply within 4 hours |
| Enterprise | Custom quote | Custom quote | 50 | Unlimited | Unlimited | Named support manager, reply within 1 hour |

All plans include unlimited projects, unlimited guest viewers and two-factor authentication. Automation runs reset on the first day of each billing month; unused runs do not carry over to the next month.

## Add-ons

| Add-on | Price | Available on |
|---|---|---|
| Extra storage | $5 per 100 GB per month | Starter, Team, Business |
| Advanced analytics | $8 per user per month | Team, Business (included in Enterprise) |
| Single sign-on (SAML SSO) | $4 per user per month | Team (included in Business and Enterprise) |
| Audit log export | $50 per workspace per month | Business (included in Enterprise) |

## Free trial

New workspaces can try the Business plan free for 14 days. No payment card is needed. At the end of the trial the workspace moves to the Starter plan unless a paid plan is chosen.

## Discounts

- Registered nonprofits: 30% off any plan billed annually.
- Schools and universities: 50% off the Team plan billed annually.
- Discounts cannot be combined, and they do not apply to add-ons.

## Changing plans

Upgrades take effect immediately and the difference is charged pro rata for the rest of the billing period. Downgrades are covered in the Refund Policy.
