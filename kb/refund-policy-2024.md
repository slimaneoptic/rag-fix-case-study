# Refund Policy

This policy applies to all Orbitask subscriptions. Effective January 15, 2024.

## Annual plans

Annual plans can be cancelled for a full refund within 14 days of purchase. After 14 days, annual plans are non-refundable, but you may cancel to stop automatic renewal.

## Monthly plans

Monthly plans are not refundable. You can cancel at any time and you keep access until the end of the month you paid for.

## Downgrades and removed users

Downgrading or removing users takes effect at the next renewal date. No credit or refund is given for the current billing period.

## How to request a refund

Email billing@orbitask.example from the workspace owner's address with your workspace name and invoice number.

## Processing time and fees

Approved refunds are processed within 10 business days. Refunds of payments made by bank transfer carry a $25 processing fee.
