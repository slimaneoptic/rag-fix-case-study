# Integrations

Orbitask connects to the tools your team already uses. Integrations are set up by a workspace owner or admin under Settings > Integrations.

## Availability by plan

| Integration | Starter | Team | Business | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Slack | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Google Drive | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Microsoft Teams | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| GitHub | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Jira (two-way sync) | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| Salesforce | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| Zapier | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Custom webhooks | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

## Notes

- The Jira import tool (see Getting Started) works on every plan; the two-way Jira sync needs the Business plan or higher.
- The Salesforce integration syncs accounts and opportunities every 15 minutes.
- Zapier and webhook actions count as automation runs.
- Integrations marked Beta are not covered by the SLA.
