# Security and Data Retention

## Encryption

Customer data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher. Encryption keys are rotated every 90 days.

## Where data is stored

Workspaces are hosted in one of two regions: the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany) or the United States (Virginia). The region is chosen when the workspace is created. Moving an existing workspace to the other region is available on the Enterprise plan only.

## Certifications

Orbitask holds a SOC 2 Type II report, renewed every year, and is GDPR compliant. A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is available to all paying customers. An independent penetration test is run once a year.

## Retention

| Data | How long it is kept |
|---|---|
| Deleted items (trash) | 30 days, then permanently deleted |
| Workspace data after the subscription ends | 90 days, then permanently deleted |
| Backups | 35 days |
| Audit logs, Team plan | 90 days |
| Audit logs, Business plan | 1 year |
| Audit logs, Enterprise plan | 7 years |

Workspace owners can export all workspace data at any time under Settings > Data export, including during the 90 days after the subscription ends.

## Reporting a security issue

Email security@orbitask.example. Security reports are answered within 2 business days.
